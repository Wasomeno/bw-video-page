02 Knowledge

Meridian

A knowledge surface that retrieves without forcing a search ritual. Teams ask in the language of the work and receive the page, not a list.

Status

Problem

Institutional knowledge was filed, tagged, and then lost to the query box. The right document existed. The path to it did not.

Approach

Meridian treats every artifact as a coordinate. Retrieval is a movement toward the source, with citations kept in the margin so trust never depends on a black box.

Outcome

Answers arrive with their origin. New operators inherit the field instead of a folder tree. Search becomes a last resort.
