06 Orchestration

Loom

A timeline where agents, humans, and systems share one sequence. Loom keeps orchestration legible while work is still moving.

Problem

Automation ran in the dark. When a thread stalled, nobody could see whether a person, a model, or a queue was holding the line.

Approach

Loom renders every run as a single strip. Handoffs are explicit. A human can step in without breaking the sequence, and the record stays complete.

Outcome

Failures have authors. Completions have a shape. Orchestration is something you can watch, pause, and resume.
