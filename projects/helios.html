Helios

A quiet operations console. Exceptions surface as a single line of truth, then recede. Operators stay with the work, not the tooling.

Problem

Incident boards were louder than the systems they watched. Status lived in five surfaces, and the first ten minutes of every event were spent reconstructing what was already known.

Approach

Helios collapses telemetry, runbooks, and ownership into one field. Intelligence ranks what matters, then hides. The console only raises its voice when a human decision is actually required.

Outcome

On-call reads one surface. Handoffs no longer rewrite the night. The product feels absent until it is needed, which is the point.
